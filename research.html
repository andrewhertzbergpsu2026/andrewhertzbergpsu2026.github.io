Research interests / AI evaluation & scalable oversight

Research interests

I’m interested in making model behavior easier to evaluate, scalable oversight, and learning what it means to align superintelligence.

Mostly beginners/exploratory work. This is not yet real/strong research, just toy projects.

Representations of message roles

My LLM Message-Role Study uses Qwen2.5-1.5B to compare activations associated with user text and tool-formatted text. I extract model activations and train simple classifiers to investigate whether the representations are distinguishable.

Computational research background

As an undergraduate research assistant at Penn State from January to August 2025, I developed and analyzed numerical ODE simulations of mRNA translation and ribosome dynamics. I tested stability and behavior across parameter regimes.

I presented the poster “Numerical Study of a Traffic Flow Inspired by DNA Transcription Modeling” at the 2025 SIAM New York–New Jersey–Pennsylvania Section Conference at Penn State, October 31–November 2.

What I’m studying

My independent study includes Stanford CS336 and Chapters 1–6 of How to Scale Your Model, including their exercises. Topics include Transformer implementation, optimization, training validation, roofline analysis, and distributed compute and memory accounting.
